NOTORYX Connect is an independent field app for LoRa mesh radios running Meshtastic® firmware. Connect your radio over Bluetooth and message, map and manage your mesh without cell towers or internet.

MESSAGING
• Channel and direct messages with delivery status
• Messages queue while your radio is disconnected and send when it reconnects
• Search your full message history, kept separately for each radio
• Optional auto-reply bot with quiet hours and strict reply limits

NODES AND MAP
• Live node list with signal, battery and hop information
• Map of node positions and neighbor links, with waypoints
• Map areas you have viewed stay available offline
• Favorites and notes for the nodes you care about

FIELD TOOLS
• Traceroute with per-hop signal, neighbor reports, range-test reception and Store & Forward history
• Incident log, check-in and assistance message templates you review before sending
• Optional coverage recording with CSV export
• Field alerts: low battery, new nodes, and favorites that go quiet

RADIO SETTINGS
• Edit owner, LoRa, channel, device, position and module settings, confirmed by reading them back from the radio
• Share and import channels by QR code or link
• Encrypted radio settings backup and restore

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no servers of ours
• Location sharing is off by default and can be rounded on your phone before it is sent
• Encrypted phone data backup for moving to a new phone, and one-tap deletion of your data

REQUIREMENTS
A LoRa radio running Meshtastic® firmware with Bluetooth. Most features need a connected radio.

Mesh radio is not a guaranteed or secure communication channel. Do not rely on this app as your only way to call for help in an emergency.
